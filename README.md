# P6-Peter-RW-Ch4

Peter · Reading & Writing Chapter 4. A colourful, tap-friendly practice game for Primary 6 English.

Theme: **charity**, **volunteering**, and **You can make a difference!**

Vocabulary and grammar questions are Traditional Chinese. Vocabulary answers stay a picture plus the English word. Verb and other English answer keys stay in English. The play, the book back cover, and the model letter stay in English. Buttons, help, section instructions, and feedback are Traditional Chinese.

## Play online

GitHub Pages is not turned on for this repository. That is on purpose: Eri approves Pages first. This project does not turn Pages on by itself.

When Pages is turned on later, use branch `main` and folder `/` (root). The address would be:

https://tradingservant.github.io/P6-Peter-RW-Ch4/

## Open locally

From this folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080

You can also open `index.html` directly in a browser. The game does not need a server or a network connection. Progress (stars) stays in the browser (`localStorage` key `p6-peter-rw-ch4-v1`).

On an iPhone, use Safari. Buttons are large. Add it to the Home Screen if you want an app icon.

Check the answer key without a browser:

```bash
node tests/answers.test.js
```

## What is inside

1. **詞彙 Vocabulary** — charity work from Book p.43. Match pictures A–I, then fill the phrases: visit the elderly, sell flags, run a second-hand stall, collect clothes, organise a charity sale, go on a charity run, donate canned food, take part in a charity show, go on a charity walk, run a charity sale.
2. **文法 Grammar** — either … or and neither … nor for Charity Week; too / not enough / since from the writing checklist; Senior Citizen Services; Tommy’s personality words (polite, gets on with, agrees with, shares, honest, told, lie to, tidied).
3. **閱讀 Play** — Kind Heart School charity-week play. Summary words and multiple choice.
4. **閱讀 Book** — back cover of *Children Don't Give Up*, then Joey’s blog.
5. **寫作 Letter** — arrange a formal letter to the Principal and fill since, too, not enough, and who.

Stars are saved on this device. Sound is optional and made in the browser (no audio files).

## Answer key

Correct answers follow the corrected Chapter 4 worksheet. Red teacher marks win over the pencil answers.

- Volunteering: the **choice** you make, the **difference** you make and the **world** you make.
- Picture match: 1F, 2B, 3G, 4H, 5D, 6I, 7E, 8A, 9C.
- Charity Week: insert **either** before the two choices. Morning raffle tickets are 9–11 a.m., so they **go on a charity run**. They do not like singing, so they **run a second-hand stall**. Neither Kelly nor I **have** free time.
- Checklist sentences: *The camps are too crowded for people to live comfortably.* *There are not enough doctors to help them all.* *Since there is not enough food, the people there are weak.* Spell **weak**, not week.
- Senior Citizen Services helps **old people** with **support and care services**, and it is important **since** it can help maintain the independence of old people.
- Tommy: polite, gets on with, agrees with, shares, honest, told, lie to, **tidied** (not tidy). take care of and argue are not used.
- Play summary, one word each: hall, bad, help, hospital, accident, **visit**, **fair**. Not visited. Not charity alone.
- Play multiple choice: 1D, 2D, 3D, 4B (*Max learns an important lesson*). The red tick on question 4 sits on B.
- Back cover: 1C (6–12-year-olds), 2D (it has **not** been printed before — that statement is the one that is not true), 3C, 4D (*How to Prevent the Flu* is the chapter you cannot find), 5D (a great choice = the book).
- Blog: **information**, **stories**. Not grass.
- Formal letter: date, Dear Principal, subject, body, **Yours faithfully**, name. The practice letter is signed Joey, a book character.
